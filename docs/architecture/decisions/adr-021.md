# ADR-021: Single-owner tanks

Version: 0.2 | Date: 19 October 2026 | Status: Accepted

Owner/approver: human owner/architect. Approval evidence: [authorised decisions, 19 October 2026](../../engineering/authorised-decisions-2026-10-19.md), recording the owner's explicit chat directions and acceptance.

## Context and options

Single owner was initially a draft assumption. Shared invitations/roles would require broader access/revocation design.

## Decision

Single-owner tanks. General aquarium tracking includes freshwater and marine. Multiple tanks remain domain-supported; no commercial tank-count limit is established.

## Consequences and verification

Test owner isolation for tank, readings, maintenance, photos and advice history. Sharing requires a future design decision. General scope does not imply universal dosing formulas or AI coverage.

## Traceability

FR-002/003; NFR-007; DEC-01/03.

## History

0.1 proposed single-owner assumption; 0.2 accepted by owner 19 October 2026. No implied acceptance of unrelated identity/entitlement settings.
