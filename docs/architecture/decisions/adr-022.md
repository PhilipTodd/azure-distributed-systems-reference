# ADR-022: Environments, cost and recovery

Version: 0.6 | Date: 20 October 2026 | Status: Partially Accepted

Owner/approver: human owner/architect. Approval evidence: [authorised decisions, 19 October 2026](../../engineering/authorised-decisions-2026-10-19.md), recording the owner's explicit chat directions and acceptance.

## Context and options

Shared-platform inventory and recovery feasibility are not verified. Alternate regions/topologies remain options where availability/cost requires review.

## Accepted decisions

Prefer Australia East for Azure resources. Budget is not set. Make explicit efforts to keep hosting costs low through appropriate resource tiers; avoid unnecessary always-on resources and duplicated retrieval services.

## Remaining proposals

Initial Local/dev-only scope and deferred Test/Production provisioning are accepted in ADR-025. Detailed environment isolation, numeric NFR/RPO/RTO targets, exact tiers, networking, scaling and recovery strategy remain unaccepted where not separately approved. No selected SKU, monetary ceiling or spending authority.

## Consequences and verification

Estimate fixed/usage costs and compare tiers against workload/availability/recovery needs. Confirm regional support. Provisioning still needs applicable human authorisation; an unset budget does not permit uncontrolled spend.

## Traceability and history

NFR-001/006/012/013; DEC-02. 0.1 was entirely proposed; 0.2 accepts preference/cost direction only. See [Azure topology](../../infrastructure/azure-topology.md).
