# ADR-010: Entitlement authority

Version: 0.1 | Date: 8 October 2026 | Status: Proposed

Owner: human owner/architect. Approver/date/evidence: pending.

## Context

Paid AI needs one server-side authority independent of browser redirects.

## Options

Separate billing service; provider queried on every request.

## Proposed decision

Aquarium owns entitlement policy and validated commercial state.

## Consequences and verification

Aquarium grows a billing module; extraction remains possible.
