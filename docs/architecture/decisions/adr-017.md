# ADR-017: Observability and privacy

Version: 0.1 | Date: 8 October 2026 | Status: Proposed

Owner: human owner/architect. Approver/date/evidence: pending.

## Context

Distributed failures need correlation without leaking tank context or credentials.

## Options

Per-service uncorrelated logs; indiscriminate full payload capture.

## Proposed decision

OpenTelemetry, App Insights/Log Analytics and redacted diagnostics; audit separately.

## Consequences and verification

Sampling/retention/cost and safe operational fields need approval.

NFR-001–006, NFR-009. See [pack index](../../engineering/architecture-pack.md), [service design](../services.md) and [decision register](../../engineering/decision-register.md).
