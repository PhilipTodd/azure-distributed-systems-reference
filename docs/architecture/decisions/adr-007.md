# ADR-007: Reliable publication

Version: 0.1 | Date: 8 October 2026 | Status: Proposed

Owner: human owner/architect. Approver/date/evidence: pending.

## Context

Writing data then publishing can lose messages.

## Options

Direct publication with reconciliation; alternate change-capture pattern.

## Proposed decision

Local SQL/Cosmos transactional outboxes and duplicate-safe consumers.

## Consequences and verification

At-least-once relay, inbox/deduplication and operational backlog recovery required.

FR-003, NFR-004. See [pack index](../../engineering/architecture-pack.md), [service design](../services.md) and [decision register](../../engineering/decision-register.md).
