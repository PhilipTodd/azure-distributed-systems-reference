# ADR-001: Three service boundaries

Version: 0.1 | Date: 8 October 2026 | Status: Proposed

Owner: human owner/architect. Approver/date/evidence: pending.

## Context

Aquarium configuration, historical tracking and AI have different responsibilities and failure/cost profiles.

## Options

A modular monolith reduces operations; more entity services increase coordination.

## Proposed decision

Use Aquarium, Tracking and Advisor bounded contexts; deploy Advisor when its release is needed.

## Consequences and verification

Adds network/operational complexity; preserve meaningful ownership rather than service count theatre.
