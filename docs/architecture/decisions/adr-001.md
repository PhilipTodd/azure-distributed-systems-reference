# ADR-001: Three service boundaries

Version: 0.2 | Date: 26 October 2026 | Status: Accepted

Owner: human owner/architect. Approver: human project owner. Approved: 26 October 2026. Evidence: owner stated in the docs-site update chat: "The proposed separation of services is approved."

## Context

Aquarium configuration, historical tracking and AI have different responsibilities and failure/cost profiles.

## Options

A modular monolith reduces operations; more entity services increase coordination.

## Decision

Use Aquarium, Tracking and Advisor bounded contexts; deploy Advisor when its release is needed.

## Consequences and verification

Adds network/operational complexity; preserve meaningful ownership rather than service count theatre.

## Traceability

FR-002–014. See [pack index](../../engineering/architecture-pack.md), [service design](../services.md) and [decision register](../../engineering/decision-register.md).

## Approval and history

0.1: initial proposal. 0.2: human owner approved separation into Aquarium, Tracking and Advisor services on 26 October 2026.

No further decision is recorded as pending within this service-separation scope. Advisor release timing and detailed commercial/module boundaries remain governed by their respective product plans and decision records. This approval does not authorise implementation, deployment or spending.
